Audio Visualiser

A compact browser audio experience with customizable visuals, persistent preferences, and an interface designed to keep the sound in focus.

The idea

This project explores how a small utility can feel expressive without becoming difficult to use. The visualiser responds to audio while keeping controls and customization close at hand.

What I built

  • Customizable visual treatment and interface controls.
  • Client-side audio interaction with a responsive rendering surface.
  • Local persistence for user preferences and saved state.

Interaction flow

Audio Input→Analyzer→Visualiser→Saved UI
